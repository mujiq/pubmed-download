## 11. Citation conventions

- Documents in this PRD cite references by the bracketed key, e.g., `[SKOS]`, `[FELLEGI-SUNTER]`.
- When a standard is versioned, the version in scope is stated in the citing document (e.g., US Core 6.1, X12 005010).
- Vendor product references ([UDMH], [HCDM], [ORACLE-BICC]) are described generically; adopting organisations hold the
  licensed documentation and should attach their licence references in the module 04 harmonisation matrix.
- Content from the *Ontology Pipeline* book is referenced at the level of its staged framework and governance principles
  only; implementers should consult the book directly for the author's rationale and detailed method.
