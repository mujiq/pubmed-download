Care Team & Roles · hybrid human-in-the-loop

The condition pathways are delivered by a primary-care team. PureScore is digital-first but human-in-the-loop: each role keeps its clinical responsibilities; the app/agent prepares, automates, prioritises and escalates — it never makes the clinical decision. Single source: data/care-roles.json.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

Reception / front desk

First contact & visual triage (esp. mental health).

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Register patient
  • Visual triage per clinic protocol
  • Book to GP (P3) or FM (P1/2)
  • Pre-fill registration from PURA profile
  • Surface a pre-computed priority tier so booking is one tap

Escalates to: care_coordinator, fm

Care Coordinator

Population search, invitation, engagement & behaviour change across ALL priorities.

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Search & stratify the cohort (H/M/L)
  • Invite via SMS/telephone; explain the review
  • Lifestyle brief (diet/exercise/smoking/weight/alcohol)
  • Behaviour-change techniques (motivational interviewing)
  • Signpost tools/resources; book appointments
  • Automated cohort search & risk stratification from the PureScore engine
  • Templated invitations + nudge scheduling (Doc 11)
  • Personalised risk illustration & decision-support content
  • Tracks engagement/adherence; flags non-responders

Escalates to: nurse, gp, fm

Nurse

Measurement, care-plan setup, self-management education.

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Record BP, weight, BMI, foot checks; arrange bloods if overdue
  • Teach home monitoring (BP, BM/CGM) & sick-day rules
  • Develop & maintain the care plan
  • Reinforce diet/activity goals; signpost resources
  • Auto-ingest home-BP / wearable / self-report into the record
  • Pre-populate the care plan from pathway templates
  • Reminder cadence for bloods/screening (monitoring schedule)

Escalates to: gp, fm

General Practitioner (GP)

Priority 3 — stable patients; review & medication.

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Review results & screening; medication review
  • Assess CVD risk (QRisk / UAE calculator)
  • Diagnose & manage mild/stable disease
  • Reinforce adherence & sick-day rules
  • Pre-computed score, trend, binding-constraint & provenance at point of care (Doc 03/05)
  • Flags out-of-date screening; surfaces canonical reference ranges
  • Drafts the medication-review checklist

Escalates to: fm, specialist

Family Physician (FM)

Priority 1-2 — complex/high-risk; optimise & refer.

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Optimise & titrate medications
  • Manage co-morbidities & complications
  • Refer to specialist when indicated; complete post-referral actions
  • Allocate next review by control & risk
  • Prioritised worklist (P1/P2 first) with the critical-marker cascade highlighted
  • Referral-criteria checks against pathway thresholds
  • Post-referral action tracking & recall scheduling

Escalates to: specialist

Dietitian

Structured nutrition & weight-management support (MDT).

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Individualised dietary plan (culturally tailored)
  • Weight-management & relapse-prevention support
  • Receives referral with diet history, activity & weight trend pre-attached
  • Digital check-ins & nudge follow-through

Escalates to: fm

Specialist (nephrology / endocrinology / cardiology / lipid / weight / mental-health)

Secondary care; assess then repatriate to primary care.

Clinical responsibilitiesApp / agent assist
  • Specialist assessment & management plan
  • Repatriate to primary care with actions
  • Structured referral packet (relevant markers, trends, meds, reconciliation status)
  • Repatriation actions flow back into the FM/GP worklist
