Consolidation plan · wiki pages & diagrams

Where the wiki repeats itself — pages or sections that overlap enough to collapse to one canonical home, so each concept has a single update site. Five agents scanned every page (generated HTML, builders, data, diagrams). Scoped to the wiki surface you manage — the numbered spec spine (Docs 01–19 markdown) is out of scope; where a page restates a spec, the fix edits the page (point at the spec), never the spec. Plan only — nothing is merged until you approve a candidate by its id. med = a standalone page or one that must preserve cross-refs / a diagram-data guard; low = generated-page render or label cleanup.

14 page & diagram merge candidates · 10 low · 4 med · plus 6 confirmed keep-separate and 6 out-of-scope (source spec / data).

Read this first
Scoped to the wiki surface you actually manage: the generated HTML pages and the diagrams. Most apparent duplication is intentional, already single-sourced data-driven VIEWS. The real page-level hazard is narrower: (a) generated pages that hand-copy tables instead of rendering from the canonical JSON; (b) framing/boilerplate restated across sibling pages; (c) a few pages that restate a spec's content (fixed by editing the page to point at the spec). Risk here is small: low = generated-page render / label cleanup; med = a standalone page or one that must preserve cross-refs / a diagram-data guard. Nothing in this scoped view edits the spec spine.
Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
