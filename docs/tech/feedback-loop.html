Live Feedback Loop — Continuous, Personalized Scoring

A working illustration of D23 / Doc 03 §2b: PureScore moves continuously with short-term behaviour. Tap a top-5 action and the number ticks up; log a setback and it trends down — and the companion dimensions (Trajectory, Early-warning, Modifiability) respond with it. Nothing here is discrete: every change is a smooth, personal-baseline-relative nudge. Bands stay as safety labels only.

Illustrative
A small synthetic engine (6 pillars, 11 markers) running the real Stage-2b math (r = clamp(r_clin + κ·tanh(z/2)), γ-mean pillars, δ-mean overall) to show the temporal personalisation the static scorer doesn't. Its constants (pillar weights, γ, δ) are sourced from the canonical data/*.json, so they can't drift. The full 12-pillar engine is the Calculation Explorer. Numbers are for demonstration, not a validated model.
