Production readiness — gap analysis

What a production-grade, patient-facing mobile app still needs beyond this methodology + backend spec (scope excludes CI/CD & deployment). missing = no spec yet; partial = policy/partial only; addressed = now specified. Sibling gap surfaces in this chapter: Clinician red-team & blind-spots and Coverage audit.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Two to treat as near-term
Colour-blind-safe status — green/yellow/red alone fails ~8% of male users; add a redundant icon/label cue. On-device security + auth — the largest truly-missing surface for PHI, and it gates app-store / medical review.

15 areas · 8 missing · 6 partial · 1 addressed.
