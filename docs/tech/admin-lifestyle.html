The validated questionnaires, their scoring, band cut-offs and pillar mapping. Full item text in Appendix C.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Locked governance item
The PHQ-9 item-9 suicidality hard rule (forces MCS critical, fires crisis pathway) is a locked safety control — it cannot be edited in a routine quarterly cycle; changing it requires a full safety review (Doc 16 §2.2).
