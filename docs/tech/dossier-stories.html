User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

The buildable backlog: 64 stories across the curated core classes, with Gherkin acceptance criteria and edge/failure cases. Grouped by subject area; open any class in the Class Explorer.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

Data & Streams

InputStream

US-INPUTSTREAM-1 — As a Ingestion Worker, I want classify an inbound wearable payload by its device class and assign the correct D22 trust tier before creating Measurement rows so that the downstream scoring engine never needs to re-derive trust from device metadata — it is stamped once at ingestion.

Acceptance:

  • Given a wearable payload with device_class='clinical_grade_device' (e.g. validated continuous BP cuff), When InputStream.classify() runs, Then all Measurement rows carry trust_tier='clinical_grade_device' and q_source in [0.85, 1.0]
  • Given a wearable payload with device_class='consumer_validated' (e.g. consumer smartwatch resting HR), When classified, Then trust_tier='consumer_validated' and q_source in [0.6, 0.8]
  • Given a wearable payload with device_class='inferential' (e.g. HRV-derived readiness score), When classified, Then trust_tier='inferential', q_source=0 (informational sentinel), and no Measurement row for this payload may contribute to a band classification or headline score
  • Given a lab HL7/FHIR payload from an accredited lab (stream='LAB'), When classified, Then trust_tier='lab_venous' and q_source=1.0 regardless of wearable device rules

Edge/failure: A device in the device registry that has lost its validation certification (e.g. removed from the validated-device list) must be downgraded from consumer_validated to inferential at the InputStream layer, retroactively flagging all future Measurements from that device — historical Measurements are not retroactively changed but a note is added to the device record · A mixed payload from a hub that aggregates both clinical-grade CGM and inferential stress scores must split into two InputStream sub-streams and stamp each Measurement row with its own trust_tier — the higher-trust device class must not bleed into the inferential metrics

US-INPUTSTREAM-2 — As a Scoring Engine, I want know the q_source confidence ceiling for each stream contributing to a patient's current data mix so that it can accurately compute the companion Confidence dimension and suppress over-confident green claims when data is predominantly from low-trust streams.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient whose marker coverage is 80% WEAR (consumer_validated) and 20% LAB, When the Confidence companion dimension is computed, Then it reflects the blended q_source ceiling and is labelled 'moderate confidence — limited lab data'
  • Given a patient with no LAB data and all markers from LIFE (survey, q_source 0.5–0.7), When a pillar score is 'green', Then it must be labelled 'low-coverage green' and the pillar coverage metric is visibly low (Doc 06 §4.3)
  • Given a GOAL stream update (user sets a new weight-loss goal), When processed, Then it does NOT create a Measurement row — it updates the patient's Goal record and re-ranks nudges; q_source is not applicable to GOAL stream events

Edge/failure: A patient who provides only LIFE (self-reported lifestyle PRO) data and no LAB or WEAR data cannot receive a red/critical classification solely from self-report (survey q_source 0.5–0.7 is insufficient to confirm a critical without clinical corroboration) — the system must issue a 'measure this' nudge and hold the marker at Watch, not cascade · If the WEAR stream is temporarily unavailable (device offline, consent withdrawn), the affected markers must fall back to their last confirmed Measurement with decayed confidence, not to a literature fallback that could mask a genuine adverse trend already in the personal baseline

US-INPUTSTREAM-3 — As a Compliance officer, I want verify that each InputStream type has the correct consent scope active before data is ingested so that PHI from genetic, reproductive, or wearable streams is never processed without the patient's active consent for that scope.

Acceptance:

  • Given a LAB payload containing ApoE genotype (genetic data), When ingested, When the patient lacks an active 'genetic_data' consent scope, Then the payload is rejected with 401 and a consent-required error — not silently dropped
  • Given a WEAR payload from a continuous CGM (clinical-grade), When the patient has active consent for 'wearable_clinical' scope, Then ingestion proceeds normally
  • Given a consent withdrawal POST /v1/patients/{id}/consent removing the 'wearable' scope, When a subsequent WEAR payload arrives, Then InputStream rejects the payload with 401 and existing Measurement rows from that stream are flagged for retention-policy review

Edge/failure: Ramadan fasting context (D21) alters the clinical interpretation of glucose readings from the CGM sub-stream — the InputStream must pass a context_flags field alongside the Measurement to allow the scoring engine to apply the IDF-DAR safety rule without modifying the raw value · A LIFE stream response that triggers a validated PRO (e.g., PHQ-9 score computed from individual item responses) must have the PRO composite written as a derived Measurement with source='derived' and trust_tier inheriting the survey floor, not a new InputStream classification

Measurement

US-MEASUREMENT-1 — As a Ingestion Worker, I want persist a new wearable SpO2 reading with its trust tier and derive q_source automatically so that the scoring engine always has a correct, trust-aware confidence value without manual intervention.

Acceptance:

  • Given source='wearable', device_grade='consumer_validated', value=95.0, When the measurement is persisted, Then trust_tier='consumer_validated', q_source=0.7 (midpoint of 0.6–0.8 range per Doc 06 §2, D22), and confidence_i(t=now)=0.7
  • Given source='wearable', device_grade='inferential', value=72 (readiness score), When persisted, Then trust_tier='inferential', q_source is set to informational-only sentinel (e.g. 0.0 effective band weight), and the measurement cannot contribute to a band classification
  • Given source='lab', device_grade=null, When persisted, Then trust_tier='lab_venous', q_source=1.0
  • Given the same patient + marker + effectiveTime + idempotency_key already exists, When re-ingested, Then the API returns 409 and no second row is created

Edge/failure: A wearable SpO2 reading of 88% with trust_tier='consumer_validated' must be stored but must NOT trigger the critical cascade without a clinical-grade confirmation — it may only raise Watch/Advisory (D22, wearable-critical-gate) · A measurement with source='literature_fallback' must never be written by the ingestion worker; it is synthesised by the scoring engine when no real measurement exists and the patient's cohort median is used as a neutral prior (Doc 06 §4.3)

US-MEASUREMENT-2 — As a Scoring Engine, I want retrieve the latest effective confidence-weighted value for each marker at scoring time, with recency decay applied so that stale measurements are automatically down-weighted and replaced by the literature fallback once they drop below the stale floor.

Acceptance:

  • Given a Measurement with effectiveTime=90 days ago and decay_class='medium' (τ≈60 days), When confidence_i(t) is computed, Then confidence_i = q_source * exp(-90/60) ≈ 0.22 for a lab measurement (q_source=1.0)
  • Given confidence_i < per-marker stale_floor, When the scoring engine resolves the marker value, Then source is set to literature_fallback, confidence is set to the literature_fallback q_source (0.2–0.4), and the pillar's coverage metric is decremented
  • Given a permanent-decay marker (Lp(a), genetics, τ=∞), When confidence_i is computed regardless of age, Then it never decays below q_source (exp(-t/∞) = 1)

Edge/failure: A CGM-derived time-in-range value (source='derived', tier='comprehensive') must carry the minimum q_source of its constituent raw CGM readings — if any constituent is consumer_validated, the derived value cannot exceed q_source=0.8 · When multiple Measurements exist for the same patient + marker within the same day (e.g., two BP readings), the scoring engine must use the most recent by effectiveTime unless the older reading has significantly higher trust_tier, in which case the implementation-defined blending rule from Doc 03 §3 applies

US-MEASUREMENT-3 — As a Data governance auditor, I want reconstruct the exact PureScore at any historical point-in-time using only the Measurement time-series so that the system is fully auditable and a score can never be improved by deleting an unfavourable measurement.

Acceptance:

  • Given all Measurement rows for a patient up to timestamp T, When the scoring engine is replayed at T, Then it produces the same PureScore that was emitted live at T (deterministic replay invariant)
  • Given a patient deletes a red Measurement record (or it is soft-deleted), When the score is recomputed, Then coverage drops and the pillar score is not improved — the reservoir layer retains the burden accumulated by that measurement (Doc 06 §5 anti-gaming, Doc 04)

Edge/failure: A measurement marked with data_quality_flag='artifact' (D14) is retained in the time-series with confidence forced to ~0; it is not deleted, and the QA audit trail records the flag reason · GDPR right-to-erasure requests must be handled by anonymisation, not deletion, to preserve time-series integrity for clinical audit — a legal hold flag prevents physical deletion until the retention period expires (Doc 16)

US-MEASUREMENT-4 — As a Personal Baseline engine, I want query the time-series for a marker to build a personal baseline distribution for early-warning z-score computation so that the z-score reflects genuine personal drift rather than population noise.

Acceptance:

  • Given >=N_min (per-marker) Measurements with confidence above a threshold, When the PersonalBaseline is built, Then mean and std are computed on the log-transformed series for skew-tagged markers (D13) and on the raw series for others
  • Given <N_min Measurements, When PersonalBaseline is requested, Then the empirical-Bayes prior (cohort mean and std) is used with full shrinkage weight (D6), and the EarlyWarning layer operates on the population baseline only until sufficient personal data accrues
  • Given a Measurement is ingested that is z>3 from the personal baseline, When confidence is full (not artifact-flagged), When the marker is critical=true, Then a confirmed critical cascade is triggered (not merely Watch)

Edge/failure: If a patient changes their wearable device mid-stream (different calibration), the PersonalBaseline must restart from the device-change date or apply a calibration offset if one is available — the personal mean/std across device generations is not directly comparable · Seasonal variation in markers (e.g. vitamin D, mood PRO scores) must not be mistaken for a trend — the baseline engine should account for annual cyclicity where flagged on the Marker definition

Markers & Bands

Marker

US-MARKER-1 — As a Scoring Engine, I want look up the band thresholds and within-pillar weight for a given marker resolved to a patient's cohort context so that it can compute the correct clinical risk score r_i without embedding band logic in the aggregator.

Acceptance:

  • Given marker_id='apob' and cohort={age:45,sex:'M',disease:[],medications:[]}, When the engine calls Marker.band(cohort), Then it returns green:<80, yellow:80-99, red:>=100 (mg/dL) per ESC/EAS and weight=0.18
  • Given the same marker and cohort={medications:['statin']}, When Marker.band(cohort) is called, Then the returned thresholds are unchanged (band reflects biology, not medication effect — MANAGED state is set separately by ManagedState, D3)
  • Given marker_id='hdl' with two_sided=true, When Marker.band(cohort={sex:'F'}) is called, Then both low-adverse (<40 mg/dL red) and high-normal (>=50 green) thresholds are returned
  • Given a guideline bump is proposed via admin-constants, When the model-version gate runs, Then the band function for affected markers is updated only after clinician approval and re-validation (D20)

Edge/failure: Sex-hormone markers (estradiol, FSH) with sex_axis='gonadal' must resolve bands from the patient's life-stage and HRT status (Doc 08, D15); a missing life-stage falls back to natal-sex defaults with confidence penalty · A marker band using cohort percentiles (HRV, VO2max) that has no reference distribution for the patient's cohort cell must fall back to the nearest super-cohort and lower confidence (D7)

US-MARKER-2 — As a Clinician admin, I want flag a marker as critical and specify its escalation rule so that the scoring engine can apply a hard pillar-cap and route a confirmed red to the crisis pathway without per-marker special-casing in the aggregator.

Acceptance:

  • Given Marker.critical=true and a confirmed measurement in the red band, When the CriticalCascade runs, Then the pillar score is capped at <=40 and an escalation event is emitted (Doc 03 §6)
  • Given PHQ-9 item-9 suicidality answer > 0, When the marker's escalation_rule='suicidality_override' fires, Then the MCS pillar is forced to red/critical regardless of total PHQ-9 score (Doc 02 §P12 hard safety rule)
  • Given Marker.critical=false (e.g. VO2max deficit), When the marker enters red, Then it is classified as a reserve-deficit red (priority-improvement) and does NOT trigger the cascade

Edge/failure: A marker straddling acute-danger and reserve-deficit semantics (SpO2) must be classified as critical=true; the data-quality layer may lower confidence to reconfirm-pending before cascade (D14) · Changing critical=true to false requires a model-version bump and governance sign-off (D20, admin-constants)

US-MARKER-3 — As a Ingestion Worker, I want validate that an incoming value is within the physiological plausibility range defined on the Marker so that out-of-range values are routed to the QA queue rather than silently corrupting the score.

Acceptance:

  • Given marker_id='potassium' with plausibility_range=[1.5, 9.0] mmol/L, When a value of 12.0 is ingested, Then the measurement is rejected with 422 implausible and enqueued for QA review
  • Given marker_id='hba1c' and value=5.2 (within range), When ingested with source='lab' and trust_tier='lab_venous', Then the measurement is persisted with q_source=1.0 and no QA flag

Edge/failure: A value within plausible range but extreme relative to the patient's personal baseline (z > 4) must be flagged 'reconfirm pending' per D14, not rejected outright — it may be a genuine acute event · A plausibility range for derived markers (HOMA-IR, FIB-4) depends on the constituent inputs being present; missing inputs must yield 422 with a structured error naming the missing components

PersonalBaseline

US-PERSONALBASELINE-1 — As a patient, I want my baseline for each marker to start from population norms and smoothly personalise as my readings accumulate so that I get honest feedback from day one without needing months of data.

Acceptance:

  • Given a new patient with zero prior readings for HRV, When the personal baseline is requested, Then μ_i^p = cohort_mean and σ_i^p = cohort_sd with shrinkage_weight = 0.0 (fully cold-start)
  • Given a patient with 30 daily HRV readings, When the baseline is updated, Then shrinkage_weight > 0.5 and μ_i^p has moved meaningfully toward the patient's empirical mean
  • Given a heavy-tailed marker (hsCRP, flagged skew=true per D13), When the baseline is updated, Then μ_i^p and σ_i^p are computed on log(value) and the z-score is on the log scale

Edge/failure: Patient with a single outlier reading early in their history: empirical-Bayes shrinkage must dampen the outlier's influence; a single extreme value should not permanently skew the baseline

US-PERSONALBASELINE-2 — As a system, I want the personal z-score to drive the Stage 2b term and correctly orient adverse direction per marker so that improvement-vs-your-baseline always lowers r_i and degradation always raises it, with correct polarity per marker.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient whose resting HR (adverse direction = high) drops below their personal mean, When Stage 2b computes z_i = sign_adverse·(x_i − μ_i^p)/σ_i^p, Then z_i < 0 and r_i^pers = κ_resp·tanh(z_i/2) < 0, reducing r_i
  • Given a patient whose HDL (adverse direction = low) rises above their personal mean, When Stage 2b runs, Then z_i < 0 and r_i decreases
  • Given z_i = 2.0 and κ_resp = 0.10, When r_i^pers is computed, Then r_i^pers = 0.10·tanh(1.0) ≈ 0.076 and the result is bounded to [−0.10, +0.10]

Edge/failure: σ_i^p → 0 (patient has near-constant readings): denominator clamp must prevent z-score explosion; minimum σ is floored to a fraction of cohort σ

US-PERSONALBASELINE-3 — As a clinician, I want to see the trajectory and early-warning signals derived from the personal baseline history so that I can detect adverse trends before any clinical band is crossed.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient whose sleep duration z-scores have been positive (worse-than-baseline) for 7 consecutive days, When the companion vector is computed, Then Trajectory for SLP is flagged as declining and an Early-warning Watch is raised
  • Given z-scores return to negative after the adverse streak, When the companion is recomputed, Then the Watch flag is downgraded (with hysteresis) and Trajectory shows recovering
  • Given the diagonal-covariance aggregate D = sqrt(Σzᵢ²) exceeds the Advisory threshold across multiple markers simultaneously, When early-warning runs, Then an Advisory (not just Watch) is surfaced per D10

Edge/failure: Patient on a new medication that systematically shifts a biomarker (e.g. statin lowers LDL): the personal baseline will appear to improve rapidly; the engine must not interpret the drug-effect step-change as an adverse-to-normal rebound — medication context (D3) should flag the reading as MANAGED with down-weight on the baseline z

US-PERSONALBASELINE-4 — As a engineer, I want each baseline update to be stored as a versioned snapshot so the history of baseline parameters is auditable so that we can replay any historical z-score computation from stored parameters.

Acceptance:

  • Given a new measurement is ingested, When the baseline updater runs, Then a new personal_baseline_snapshot row is inserted with updated_at = now() and the prior row is not modified
  • Given a request to audit a past PureScore, When the audit lookup resolves personal_baseline_snapshot rows by patient_id + marker_id + updated_at ≤ score.computed_at, Then the parameters at the time of the score are recovered exactly
  • Given the baseline for a skew-tagged marker, When the row is read, Then log_scale = true and mu and sigma are stored in log-space

Edge/failure: Bulk historical data upload (e.g. 2 years of wearable data imported on onboarding): baseline must be computed in chronological order over the import batch; out-of-order processing must be detected and rejected or re-sorted

Scoring Engine

Pillar

US-PILLAR-1 — As a patient, I want the worst marker in a pillar to dominate the pillar score even when my other markers in that pillar are green so that a single dangerous value cannot be averaged away and hidden.

Acceptance:

  • Given a CV pillar with systolic BP r=0.7 (red) and three other markers all r=0.05 (green), When pillar aggregation runs with γ=3, Then R_CV is substantially closer to 0.7 than to the simple average of all four markers
  • Given R_k ≥ 0.60, When status is computed, Then status_k = critical regardless of whether a specific critical-marker flag is set
  • Given a critical-marker (e.g. eGFR) with r_i ≥ 0.5 in REN pillar, When status is computed, Then status_k = critical and R_k is floored to max(R_k, R_crit=0.60)

Edge/failure: All markers in a pillar are imputed (confidence low): coverage cov_k < threshold → pillar displayed as low-coverage-green, not confirmed-green; S_k is shown with a data-gap caveat

US-PILLAR-2 — As a clinician, I want to see pillar coverage alongside the score so I know when a green pillar is green by default vs evidenced so that I do not over-interpret a pillar score based on absent data.

Acceptance:

  • Given a pillar with 3 of 6 markers observed and the rest literature-imputed, When GET /v1/patients/{id}/pillars is called, Then cov_k = observed_weight / total_weight < 1.0 and a low_coverage flag is set
  • Given cov_k is below the green-display threshold, When the pillar is returned, Then status may not be displayed as confirmed-green even if R_k < 0.15
  • Given all markers are observed with full confidence, When coverage is computed, Then cov_k = 1.0 and no caveat is added

Edge/failure: Marker added to a pillar mid-model-version: historical pillar snapshots retain the old coverage denominator; the new marker only affects snapshots after the model-version bump

US-PILLAR-3 — As a system, I want the reservoir load term to raise a pillar's risk even when its spot markers look acceptable so that chronic burden (e.g. accumulated sleep debt on SLP/CV) is reflected in the pillar risk continuously.

Acceptance:

  • Given B̃_SLP = 0.8 (high chronic sleep debt) and ρ_SLP = 0.2, When pillar risk is computed, Then R_SLP = clamp(R_SLP^mark + 0.2·0.8) = clamp(R_SLP^mark + 0.16) which is ≥ R_SLP^mark
  • Given ρ_k = 0.20 and B̃_k = 1.0, When the reservoir term is added, Then the maximum reservoir contribution is 0.20 and R_k is clamped to [0,1]
  • Given reservoir load is zero (B̃_k=0), When pillar risk is computed, Then R_k = R_k^mark with no reservoir contribution

Edge/failure: A pillar receives contributions from multiple reservoirs (e.g. ALLO and SLP both feed MCS): B̃_k is the aggregate normalized load; individual reservoir contributions must be decomposed in the explanation for nudge attribution

PureScore

US-PURESCORE-1 — As a patient, I want my PureScore to be immediately capped at or below 40 whenever a life-threatening biomarker is critical so that I and my clinician see unambiguous urgency and cannot be falsely reassured by healthy pillars averaging away the danger.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient with K⁺=6.3 and all other pillars green, When the scoring engine runs, Then PureScore ≤ 40 and overall_status = CRITICAL
  • Given a patient where the critical marker is flagged as a lab artifact (confidence≈0 per D14), When the engine runs, Then the cascade does NOT fire and PureScore is computed normally with a Reconfirm-pending flag
  • Given a patient whose K⁺ drops below the red threshold on re-test, When the engine runs without confirming-measurement hysteresis cleared, Then PureScore remains capped until hysteresis resolves

Edge/failure: Two simultaneous critical markers in different pillars: cascade must still cap at 40 and surface both binding constraints in the explanation · Model-version change mid-flight: snapshot must record the model_version_id at the time of computation so audits are reproducible

US-PURESCORE-2 — As a patient, I want my PureScore to tick up visibly the day after I get a good night's sleep or complete 9,000 steps so that daily positive actions produce real, immediate feedback reinforcing the behaviour loop.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient's personal baseline for sleep duration is μ=6.5h and they log 8h, When the scoring engine runs Stage 2b, Then r_i^pers = κ_resp·tanh(z/2) with z<0 and PureScore rises by a measurable non-zero delta
  • Given κ_resp=0.10 and band_clamp active, When the personal-baseline term is computed, Then the resulting r_i never falls below the Stage 2 clinical/cohort floor (band_clamp cannot relax a red)
  • Given the pillar is already red/critical, When Stage 2b runs, Then the 𝟙[non-critical] flag suppresses the personal-baseline additive term entirely

Edge/failure: Cold-start patient with fewer than the minimum measurement count: empirical-Bayes shrinks to cohort prior; z-score weight is low so feedback signal is small but still non-zero

US-PURESCORE-3 — As a clinician, I want to retrieve the binding constraint (which pillar and marker capped the score) with every score fetch so that I can immediately focus on the correct clinical concern without reading the full explanation object.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient with a critical REN pillar, When GET /v1/patients/{id}/score is called, Then the response includes cappedBy: {pillar:'REN', marker:'K+', escalation:'emergency'}
  • Given all pillars are on-track, When GET /v1/patients/{id}/score is called, Then cappedBy is null and overall_status = ON_TRACK
  • Given overall_status = AT_RISK (R_total ≥ 0.30 but no critical), When score is fetched, Then cappedBy is null and PureScore = PureScore° without cascade

Edge/failure: Score requested before any measurements exist: return 404 or a coverage=0 sentinel score clearly marked insufficient-data, never a fabricated value

US-PURESCORE-4 — As a engineer, I want every PureScore snapshot to be deterministic and immutable given its inputs and model version so that audit trails and regulatory reviews can reproduce any historical score exactly.

Acceptance:

  • Given identical input measurements and the same model_version_id, When the scoring engine runs twice, Then both snapshots have identical value, status, and explanation JSON
  • Given a constant change triggers a model-version bump per D20, When the new version is applied, Then old snapshots retain the old model_version_id and are not retroactively altered
  • Given admin-constants is called with a valid proposal, When the gate passes, Then a new model_version record is created and all subsequent score snapshots reference it

Edge/failure: Concurrent ingest events for the same patient within the same second: idempotency-key dedup must prevent double-computation; last-write-wins with the newer measurement timestamp

CriticalCascade

US-CRITICALCASCADE-1 — As a system, I want a confirmed acute-danger critical marker to unconditionally trigger the emergency escalation pathway so that no clinical emergency can be silenced by healthy pillars or a high background PureScore.

Acceptance:

  • Given K⁺=6.3 with escalation=emergency and confidence=1.0, When CriticalCascade runs, Then overall_status=CRITICAL, PureScore≤40, and the emergency pathway (Doc 16) is triggered within the same compute cycle
  • Given SpO2=88% from a consumer-tier wearable (confidence≈0 per D14/D22), When CriticalCascade runs, Then the cascade does NOT fire; an Early-warning Watch is raised instead
  • Given SpO2=88% corroborated by a second clinical-grade device (confidence=1.0), When CriticalCascade runs, Then the cascade fires and escalation=emergency is routed to the clinician

Edge/failure: Emergency escalation must fire even if the patient has previously opted out of notifications: the safety pathway bypasses nudge-preference settings (Doc 16 compliance)

US-CRITICALCASCADE-2 — As a patient, I want the cascade to lift when I re-test and the critical marker is confirmed normal, but not instantaneously flip back on noise so that my score recovers honestly when the danger resolves without oscillating on measurement noise.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient exiting a critical state (K⁺ returned to 5.0), When the confirming measurement arrives, Then the cascade flag is removed only after hysteresis clearance (either confirming-measurement or sustained reservoir drain per Doc 03 §6)
  • Given only one in-range re-test with no sustained improvement, When CriticalCascade evaluates, Then status remains critical-pending-confirmation and PureScore stays capped
  • Given two consecutive in-range measurements with no adverse reservoir state, When CriticalCascade evaluates, Then the floor is lifted and PureScore rebounds to PureScore°

Edge/failure: Anti-flap: a borderline marker oscillating around the critical threshold (r=0.49/0.51) must require hysteresis to settle before status toggles — bare threshold crossing must not cause same-day flip/unflip

US-CRITICALCASCADE-3 — As a engineer, I want to distinguish reserve-deficit critical markers from acute-danger critical markers in the cascade output so that the engine routes reserve-deficit reds to improvement plans rather than the emergency pathway.

Acceptance:

  • Given VO2max in the very-low range (reserve-deficit critical, escalation=routine), When CriticalCascade runs, Then pillar is floored to R_crit, PureScore≤40, but escalation=routine and the improvement plan path (Doc 09) is triggered — NOT emergency
  • Given PHQ-9 item 9 score ≥ 3 (suicidality, escalation=emergency), When CriticalCascade runs, Then escalation=emergency and the crisis pathway is triggered immediately
  • Given escalation field missing from the marker catalogue, When CriticalCascade runs, Then computation halts and returns an error — untagged critical markers are forbidden

Edge/failure: Multiple simultaneous critical markers with mixed escalation levels: the highest escalation level (emergency > urgent > routine) governs the pathway fired; all critical markers are listed in the explanation

Context (2.0)

CompanionVector

US-COMPANIONVECTOR-1 — As a patient app, I want display a Confidence percentage alongside PureScore so users understand how much to trust the current number so that a score backed by one stale lab reads differently from a score backed by a full fresh panel, without hiding the number entirely.

Acceptance:

  • Given a pillar has coverage 0.4, mean input confidence 0.7, and low volatility (stability 0.9), When Cf_k is computed, Then Cf_k = 0.4*0.7*0.9 = 0.252, displayed as 25%
  • Given a wearable-only pillar uses a consumer-validated device (tier discount applied), When Cf_k is computed, Then the per-input trust flag from D22 is propagated into meanConf_k, reducing Confidence
  • Given Su_k < 0.50 (tau_su threshold), When the companion is returned, Then the response includes an INSUFFICIENT flag on that pillar's sufficiency field

Edge/failure: A pillar can have Su_k = 1.0 (all data present) but Cf_k < 0.3 (noisy device); the two must be surfaced independently

  • Given a PRO instrument Response (composite score), When posted, Then the Response record stores the composite_score field alongside the individual item answers for audit

Edge/failure: A bank version bump changes the weight for a question that a patient answered 3 months ago; the historical Response must retain its original deltas unchanged while new Responses use the new weights

US-RESPONSE-3 — As a patient, I want periodic and dynamic questions to be refreshed when my answer is stale so that my score reflects my current lifestyle rather than answers I gave a year ago.

Acceptance:

  • Given a Response for a refresh='periodic' cadence='quarterly' question older than 90 days, When next-question is evaluated, Then that question re-enters the eligible candidate set
  • Given a Response for a refresh='dynamic' question (e.g. current weight) and a wearable stream provides a fresher value within cadence, When next-question is evaluated, Then the question is auto-answered from the wearable and not re-prompted
  • Given a Response for a refresh='static' question (e.g. sex), When next-question is evaluated at any future date, Then that question is never re-prompted except via a supervised correction flow
  • Given a refresh='event' question (on-trigger cadence) where the trigger has re-fired (e.g. new pregnancy life-stage detected), When next-question is evaluated, Then the question re-enters the eligible set despite a prior Response existing

Edge/failure: Patient answers a 'quarterly' question on day 1, then on day 45 a wearable anomaly re-triggers the on-trigger variant of the same underlying question — the system must treat the on-trigger cadence variant as a distinct eligibility path, not blocked by the periodic Response

US-RESPONSE-4 — As a compliance layer, I want every Response to carry an immutable audit record of what weight was applied and why so that a regulatory audit can reconstruct any patient's score delta from first principles.

Acceptance:

  • Given any Response is persisted, When queried, Then it exposes patient_id, question_ref, bank_version_id, answer_label, raw_value, applied_pillar_deltas, applied_reservoir_deltas, answered_at, and idempotency_key
  • Given a Response is posted, When persisted, Then no field except soft-delete flags may ever be mutated (all changes create new Responses with correction_reason)
  • Given a PRO trigger fires alongside a Response, When the audit record is written, Then it includes triggered_pro_ref so the escalation chain is traceable
  • Given get-explanation is called for a pillar, When it traces contributing Responses, Then each Response is cited by ref, answered_at, and the delta it contributed

Edge/failure: Patient withdraws consent mid-session — all Responses must be soft-deleted (anonymised) but the aggregate deltas already applied to the score must remain traceable in the audit store under a pseudonymous token

Personas & Lifestyles

ClinicalPersona

US-CLINICALPERSONA-1 — As a scoring engine, I want to derive a patient's active clinical persona(s) from labs, wearables, and validated PRO responses so that pillar weight multipliers and action eligibility reflect the patient's true clinical context, not just their self-report.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient with HbA1c 6.1%, waist >90 cm, and HOMA-IR elevated, When the persona posterior is computed, Then 'prediabetic' has the highest softmax weight and MET multiplier 1.6 is applied
  • Given a patient with a natal-sex prior of female and age 58 with FSH elevated, When the persona matrix is evaluated, Then 'menopause' is eligible and ENDO/BCM/CV multipliers are applied (D15/D25)
  • Given a patient with FH tag and ApoB > 190 mg/dL unresponsive to lifestyle, When persona assignment runs, Then 'fh' persona is active, CV multiplier 1.5 applied, and ApoB modifiability flagged low
  • Given a patient with eGFR 38 (CKD stage 3b), When persona posterior is computed, Then 'ckd' persona is active, REN weight elevated, and NUT-PROT contraindication key is set for SafetyFilter

Edge/failure: A patient may hold 'prediabetic' and 'shift_worker' simultaneously; weight multipliers from both personas are composed multiplicatively, capped at a maximum per-pillar multiplier to prevent runaway amplification

US-CLINICALPERSONA-2 — As a NudgeEngine, I want to use persona-level weight multipliers when estimating ΔPureScore for a candidate action so that the same action (e.g. ACT-STEPS) yields a larger estimated ΔPureScore for a patient where the relevant pillar is amplified.

Acceptance:

  • Given a 'south_asian_metabolic' persona with MET multiplier 1.4, When ACT-STEPS impact is estimated, Then the MET pillar credit is 1.4× the baseline credit for an unweighted patient
  • Given an 'endurance_athlete' persona with FIT multiplier 0.8 (adaptive lows), When FIT-targeted actions are ranked, Then their ΔPureScore@30d is suppressed relative to a sedentary patient, reducing their rank (D7)
  • Given a 'frail_elderly' persona with BCM multiplier 1.4, When ACT-RES is safety-checked, Then it is surfaced as 'supervised only' and the BCM pillar credit is amplified accordingly

Edge/failure: Persona weight multipliers are applied during the impact chain-rule computation, not as a post-hoc scalar — ensuring the honesty bound (§2.5 exact recompute) is maintained with persona context

US-CLINICALPERSONA-3 — As a governance reviewer, I want persona assignments to be auditable and never used to worsen insurance price or restrict access so that the actuarial firewall is enforced and personas serve clinical benefit only.

Acceptance:

  • Given a persona assignment event, When it is written, Then an audit record is created with the evidence signals that drove the softmax posterior
  • Given a patient who has not consented to actuarial use, When the ActuarialLayer queries persona data, Then persona assignment records are excluded from the actuarial feed (post-consent gate)
  • Given any persona assignment, When it is evaluated, Then no persona code is used to raise a patient's insurance premium or restrict clinical access (no-protected-class rule, D18)

Edge/failure: Cold-start (no labs): persona posterior defaults to uninformative prior; 'healthy' archetype is assigned with low confidence; weight multipliers revert to 1.0 until evidence accumulates

PerceivedVsActual

US-PERCEIVEDVSACTUAL-1 — As a NudgeEngine, I want to detect when a patient over-estimates their fitness level so the nudge is framed as data-led awareness rather than judgment so that the patient is not shamed but is gently confronted with objective evidence that unlocks engagement.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient self-reporting 'active' on Q_CORE_SELF_PERSONA but wearable MVPA < 75 min/week, When the gap is computed for axis 'activity', Then gap_direction = 'overconfident_fitness' and nudge_strategy = 'show data gently; one concrete movement target; no shaming'
  • Given the overconfident_fitness gap is active, When the NudgeEngine selects ACT-STEPS, Then the card framing key is set to 'awareness-first' and includes the wearable MVPA figure
  • Given the gap resolves (MVPA rises to match self-report) on a subsequent measurement, When the gap is recomputed, Then gap_direction is cleared and nudge_strategy reverts to standard

Edge/failure: If wearable data is absent (low trust tier), the gap on the activity axis cannot be computed with confidence; gap_confidence is set low and the awareness-first framing is withheld to avoid false confrontation (D22)

US-PERCEIVEDVSACTUAL-2 — As a companion vector, I want the hidden_metabolic gap to raise the Early-warning dimension and trigger an awareness nudge so that a patient who feels well but has pre-diabetes in labs receives actionable, non-alarming awareness before the condition worsens.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient reporting 'optimal' metabolic health but HbA1c 5.8% and waist above threshold, When the gap for axis 'metabolic_state' is computed, Then gap_direction = 'hidden_metabolic' and companion Early-warning is raised to Watch tier
  • Given hidden_metabolic gap and companion Early-warning = Watch, When the daily nudge plan is built, Then the plan includes a MEASURE action (confirmatory test) ranked in the top-2 alongside the highest-yield glycemic lifestyle action
  • Given the gap is hidden_metabolic and HbA1c subsequently normalizes, When gap is recomputed, Then gap_direction is cleared and Early-warning Watch is withdrawn

Edge/failure: If the patient's HbA1c is imputed (low coverage), the hidden_metabolic gap is flagged with a low-confidence caveat and the companion Confidence dimension is reduced, not the Early-warning — preserving sensitivity while being honest about uncertainty

US-PERCEIVEDVSACTUAL-3 — As a overtrainer patient, I want the gap to detect that my perceived peak fitness contradicts my poor HRV and sleep debt, and prescribe recovery so that I do not continue training into injury or burnout.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient with high self-rated fitness but wearable HRV in the lowest tertile for their cohort and sleep debt flagged, When axis 'activity' gap is computed, Then gap_direction = 'overtrainer' and nudge_strategy = 'validate effort, prescribe deload/recovery; frame rest as performance'
  • Given the overtrainer gap is active, When the NudgeEngine builds the plan, Then a SLEEP recovery action (SLP-WIND or SLP-REG) is elevated in ranking and ACT-Z2 effort-cap is tightened
  • Given a patient denies stress but HRV is low and cortisol flat, When axis 'stress_load' gap is computed, Then gap_direction = 'stoic_underreporter' and the companion MCS/ENDO attention flags are raised without surfacing a stress label to the patient unprompted

Edge/failure: Wearable HRV is consumer-tier (D22); the overtrainer gap requires corroboration from at least two signals (HRV + sleep debt, or HRV + cortisol proxy) before nudge framing is changed

US-PERCEIVEDVSACTUAL-4 — As a catastrophizer patient, I want the gap to detect that my self-reported poor health contradicts green labs, and receive reassurance rather than more alarm so that health anxiety is not amplified by the app surfacing more warnings.

Acceptance:

  • Given a patient rating their health 'poor' or 'very poor' but all pillars green, When the gap for axis 'condition_load' is computed, Then gap_direction = 'catastrophizer' and nudge_strategy = 'reassurance + validation; redirect anxiety into a small mastery action; consider GAD-7'
  • Given the catastrophizer gap is active, When the NudgeEngine builds the plan, Then the plan leads with a low-effort mastery action and suppresses any 'early-warning' framing that would increase alarm
  • Given the catastrophizer framing is active, When the MCS pillar has a borderline marker, Then the companion Confidence dimension is raised (data is reliable, patient is well) rather than surfacing an advisory

Edge/failure: If a pillar that was previously green genuinely turns yellow while catastrophizer framing is active, safety dominates: the clinical signal is shown truthfully and the catastrophizer framing is suspended for that pillar
