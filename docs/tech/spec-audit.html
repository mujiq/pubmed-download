Spec build-readiness audit

A per-chapter content audit of the source specs (Docs 01–19) asking one question: could a competent engineer implement exactly what is written, without guessing? This is a different axis from the Production-readiness page (which tracks the product/app surface — auth, offline, analytics). Here the subject is the methodology spec itself. blocker = cannot implement as written; major = ambiguous / likely-wrong guess; minor = clarity or edge case.

106 findings · 26 blockers · 51 major · 29 minor · 4 already fixed · across 6 chapters.

The systemic blocker
Most blockers are not structural — the equations, gates and state machine are specified. They are numeric: coefficients, thresholds and reservoir constants (κ, λ, shrinkage k, calibration tolerances, clinical-score coefficients, pricing relativities) are repeatedly “illustrative” or “awaits real cohort data” (Doc 17 §K, Doc 19, Doc 13). So the work splits cleanly into two classes:
Resolution classWhat it coversWho closes it
Authorable nowUndefined formulas that should simply be written out, enum / naming reconciliations, dangling references, missing edge-case rules — no new data needed.Spec authors
Needs calibration dataEvery numeric constant deferred to fitting on a consented cohort, plus the clinical-score coefficient sets and actuarial relativities.Data science + the Doc 13/14 harness, gated by counsel/clinical sign-off where flagged
Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
