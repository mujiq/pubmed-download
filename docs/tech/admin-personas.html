Review — Personas & Cohort Frames

PureScore — Clinical Configuration Board
Persona / cohort-frame review · Q3 2026 review cycle
Signed in as Dr. A. Rahman
Chair, Clinical Governance · review & sign-off

Cohort frames adjust interpretation (ranges, weight multipliers, confidence) without ever relaxing the clinical safety anchor. Detail in Appendix D; sex frames Doc 08.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
