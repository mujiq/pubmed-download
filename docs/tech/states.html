Patient Life-State Machine — the Uber statechart

One mega nested statechart of every life state a patient can occupy — three concurrent regions (Care status, Life stage, Engagement) with all eight sub-machines expanded inline: acute-event (Doc 09 A0–A3), chronic management, pregnancy/reproductive & menopause & andropause (Doc 08), aging/frailty, onboarding, and the crisis pathway (Doc 16). Wheel = zoom · drag = pan · double-click a box = zoom to that section · ⛶ fullscreen. Pick a persona to light up the live state-vector; filter by sex / age / condition to prune impossible states; switch detail level to tame density.

A design-spec statechart, not a validated care pathway (re-verify before production, README §5.6). Engagement-artifact machines (nudge, check-in, self-report, adherence) are on the Engagement state machines page; this page is the human.

Care-pathway enrollment & progression

How the condition pathways overlay the Care status region: a patient is enrolled, stratified to a priority tier, moves between management states, and either escalates to a specialist or progresses to the next pathway (e.g. prediabetes → diabetes via CDS-PRE-03).

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Identified: eligibility / risk score
  Identified --> Enrolled: invited & booked
  Enrolled --> Stratified: priority tier (P1/P2/P3)
  Stratified --> Managed_FM: P1-2 · Family Physician
  Stratified --> Managed_GP: P3 · GP
  Managed_FM --> Specialist: referral criteria
  Specialist --> Managed_FM: repatriated + actions
  Managed_FM --> Monitored: optimised + care plan
  Managed_GP --> Monitored: stable + reinforced
  Monitored --> Stratified: review interval / worsening
  Monitored --> Progressed: threshold crossed (e.g. HbA1c >=6.5%)
  Progressed --> Identified: re-enrol next pathway
  Monitored --> Stable: targets met
  Stable --> [*]
