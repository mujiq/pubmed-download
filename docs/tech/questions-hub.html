Questions & Intake — overview

Every question PureScore can ask, unified into 5 content sections × 3 phases and gated so a patient is only ever asked what is relevant. The raw items live in the bank (Appendix E, 354) and the adherence check-ins (Appendix H, 41); validated PROs in Appendix C. The Eligibility & gating page shows the rules that connect onboarding answers to which questions unlock.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
