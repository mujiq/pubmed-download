Units & SI conversion

Canonical unit is SI (UK/ESC/Gulf convention); the US conventional unit is shown alongside. Every inbound feed is converted to SI before scoring, so a UK/Gulf lab reporting mmol/mol or mmol/L is scored correctly. Source of truth: data/units.json. Lp(a) is intentionally non-convertible (store the native assay unit).

Cross-cutting modifiers

ModifierSourceEffect
Smoking / nicotinePRO + cotininemultiplies CV, HEM/respiratory, INF risk
AlcoholPROmodifies HEP, MET, MCS, CV
Respiratory (SpO₂, FEV1, smoking)mixedfolds into HEM oxygen-transport + CV; severe values escalate independently
Adherence / engagementbehaviouralnot scored as health; feeds the nudge feasibility model (Doc 11) and reservoir inflows (Doc 04)

★ = critical marker — a red value can make its pillar critical and cascade PureScore into the critical band (Doc 03 §4). Sex-specific overrides: Doc 08.
