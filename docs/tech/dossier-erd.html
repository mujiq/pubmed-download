Data Model (ERD)

Production persistence sketch: one table per class (core classes hand-modelled; the rest auto-skeletoned from the spec). Time-series tables are partitioned; audit tables append-only. LIFE-stream and reservoir history are the main time-series stores.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

input_stream_event is an envelope/audit record; the Measurement table is the data of record. GOAL stream events do not produce Measurement rows — they produce Goal updates and are recorded here for consent audit only. Rejected events are retained with status='rejected' and rejection_reason for compliance audit. The trust_tier_resolved and q_source_ceiling columns are the single source of truth for stream trust — they must never be overridden downstream without a new event record.
