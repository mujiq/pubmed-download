Appendix F — Lifestyles, Axes & Perceived-vs-Actual

Any person = a vector over independent lifestyle axes. Named archetypes are common presets (nearest-vector). The PERCEIVED persona (self-report: Q_CORE_SELF_PERSONA, Q_CORE_SELF_HEALTH, goal/PRO answers) is compared with the ACTUAL persona derived from labs + wearables + validated answers; the gap drives nudge strategy (Doc 07) and adherence framing.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

Perceived-vs-actual gaps → nudge strategy

Method
For each axis, compute perceived_level from self-report (question bank GOAL/perceived items) and actual_level from objective signals (labs + wearables + validated PROs). gap = perceived_level − actual_level. The signed gap selects a nudge/communication strategy and adjusts the companion vector (Doc 12: Representativeness, Modifiability, Confidence). Large positive gap (over-estimates own health) → awareness-first; large negative gap (catastrophizing) → reassurance-first.
