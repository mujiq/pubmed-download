Editorial & cohesion review

A page-by-page comb-through of the whole wiki (six review agents, one per chapter cluster) plus a mechanical link/anchor scan, asking a single question: does this read as one cohesive product for clinicians, developers and product? It is a third axis, distinct from Spec build-readiness (can an engineer build it?) and Production readiness (what does the app still need?). high = visibly wrong or breaks the “one product” feel; med = an inconsistency a reader will notice; low = polish.

88 findings · 17 high · 41 med · 30 low · 27 already fixed · across 8 areas.

How to use this
Per your direction these are tracked, not yet applied — except a handful of defects in the newly-added chapter landing pages (marked fixed), which were corrected in place. Every fix lives in the source / builders (../purescore/*.md, wiki_content.py, build_wiki.py, data/*.json), never the generated HTML. The biggest wins are the cross-cutting items at the top: relabelling the “README” links and filling the glossary fix dozens of pages at once.
Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
