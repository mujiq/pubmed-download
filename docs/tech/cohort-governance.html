Cohort Fallback — Governance & Safeguards · round 2: make degradation safe & correct

The graceful-degradation model substitutes cohort statistics for missing data. This page is the governance around that: how the cohort is built (k-anonymity, back-off, drift, out-of-distribution), how missing values are imputed (conditional, not marginal), the equity, longitudinal-stability, integration/provenance and UAE-locale safeguards. Single source: data/cohort-governance.json.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Locked decisions (round 2)
Equity — clinical-reference scoring lockedWhen a demographic cohort median falls OUTSIDE the healthy clinical range, the filled marker is scored against the clinical/guideline target, not the demographic median — a cohort estimate can never read as reassurance for a high-risk group.
Privacy — cohort-fill everyone lockedThe cohort is built from anonymous, aggregated, k-anonymous statistics requiring no per-user consent; all users may be cohort-filled, disclosed in the privacy notice. Consent-to-contribute is separated from use-of-aggregate.
Backfill — provisional → finalized lockedCohort-filled days are PROVISIONAL; when real data backfills, the day is recomputed and FINALIZED, the original kept in an immutable ledger, and the user/clinician sees 'revised on <date>'. Hard alerts fire only on finalized/real data.
Floor — population-estimate-only state lockedBelow a real-data floor the system stops presenting a personal numeric score and switches to 'insufficient personal data — population estimate only', freezing trend/alerts until real data returns.

1 · Cohort construction

BasisAnonymous aggregate statistics (decision: cohort-fill everyone); no identifiable per-user data in the substituted value.
Minimum cell sizek ≥ 20 effective members before an age×sex×life-stage cell is usable; smaller cells are suppressed (statistical noise + re-identification risk).
Hierarchical back-offWhen the exact cell is empty/under-powered, back off age×sex×life-stage → age×sex → sex → global, with shrinkage; tag the granularity actually used and penalise Accuracy more for coarser levels.
Versioning & driftEvery cohort distribution is versioned (hash, build date, source N); each score pins the version used; periodic rebuilds; a PSI/KL drift monitor flags stale cohorts vs incoming real data.
OOD detectionA Mahalanobis typicality distance to the nearest cell (on observed markers) flags out-of-distribution users; above threshold the system withholds cohort-fill, floors Accuracy, and shows 'reference population may not represent you'.
Small-cell exposureAny cohort-derived metric surfaced in the companion vector (Cohort-fill %, Accuracy) is quantised/suppressed when the underlying cell is below the k floor — transparency must not become a re-identification side-channel.

2 · Conditional imputation & imputability

Conditional, not marginalImputed value = E[missing | observed markers, demographics] from the cohort covariance — never the bare marginal median when the user has correlated markers (don't impute a normal LDL while their measured ApoB/HbA1c say dyslipidemic). Divergence from observed correlates is capped; uncertainty widened.
Joint coherenceCorrelated markers are imputed jointly; a cross-marker plausibility check damps pillars whose fills are mutually inconsistent; a cap limits how many filled markers may jointly drive one pillar.
Imputability classesEach input is tagged cohort-fillable / conditional-only / never-fill. Never-fill = discrete clinical events, specific mutations, goals, and eligibility GATES — these drop to Sufficiency reduction + a nudge, never a substituted value.
Regression-to-mean guardFills are regularised toward the user's OWN observed extremity, never the population mean; a filled marker can only WIDEN a pillar's band — it can never reduce the risk level below what real measurements alone support.

3 · Equity safeguards

Clinical reference for unhealthy cohorts lockedDECISION — when the demographic median sits outside the healthy clinical range, score the fill against the clinical/guideline target, so cohort-fill never normalises population pathology as health for already-disadvantaged groups.
Disparate-accuracy auditPer-group distributions of fill %, Accuracy, calibration error and CTA burden are tracked against fairness thresholds; breaches alert and feed back into back-off and OOD policy.

4 · Longitudinal & stability

Aging-vs-worsening attributionΔscore is decomposed into Δ(real signal) + Δ(cohort-regression); the user sees 'X of the Y-point move is because data is N days old, not a health change', with the cohort-pull shown separately on the trend.
HysteresisOnce real, the last real value is held with a slow decay toward cohort over the staleness window; a new fill-state must persist N consecutive days before the displayed score reflects it — no day-to-day jitter from intermittent sync.
Population-estimate-only floor lockedDECISION — below a real-data fraction floor the personal numeric score is withdrawn for an explicit 'population estimate only' state; trend/alerts freeze until real data returns.
Recovery framingOn resync the upward jump is attributed to data freshness ('the previous value was a population estimate'), animating the cohort→personal transition — not read as a health change.

5 · System integration & provenance

Gates are never imputedEligibility GATES (pregnancy, mobility, smoking — they unlock/suppress whole branches) are never cohort-filled; a missing gate freezes its downstream branch as 'eligibility-pending', excluded from the score with its own nudge. Only LEAF inputs regress to cohort.
Reservoir uncertainty propagationA per-reservoir real-fraction is carried through the 15-reservoir coupling matrix; a derived reservoir's Accuracy is a function of its inputs' weighted real-fraction, and couplings driven by cohort-only inputs are damped so cohort values can't move a derived reservoir across a threshold (no uncertainty laundering).
Backfill provisional → finalized lockedDECISION — cohort-filled days are provisional; backfill recomputes and finalizes, keeping the original in an immutable ledger ('revised on <date>'); hard alerts fire only on finalized/real data.
Provenance ledgerAn append-only per-input ledger (value, source, real|cohort, cohort-version, timestamp, last-real-at) backs a 'What's real?' panel and is exportable for audit/residency review.
Clinician overlayThe clinician view badges every value real / cohort-estimated / stale-N-days inline and requires an explicit 'acknowledge estimated inputs' before any cohort-influenced value can be used in an order/decision — logged, to bound medico-legal exposure.

6 · Delivery & locale (UAE)

Unified nudge auctionData-completeness CTAs and behaviour-change nudges share ONE daily auction scored on expected clinical value; completeness nudges are capped per week, suppressed when a health-action nudge is pending, and never fired for an input below a ΔAccuracy leverage threshold — no fatigue, no crowding-out of clinical nudges.
Ramadan / fasting-awareFasting-window cohort strata + suppression of baseline-deviation alarms during declared fasting; a user fasting flag; nudge timing shifted out of fasting/prayer/iftar windows — so fasting isn't misread as decline.
Arabic / RTL & residencyAll provenance, Accuracy and forecast-cone explanations are Arabic/RTL-mirrored with correct bidi numeric handling; cohort strata are computed and stored in-region (UAE data residency), documented in the provenance ledger.
