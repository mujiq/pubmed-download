Condition Care Pathways · from score to managed care

Where the PureScore engine meets the clinic. Each condition runs an Identify → Engage → Assess → Transform lifecycle, stratifies patients into a 3-tier priority routed to a care-team role (Priority 1-2 → Family Physician, Priority 3 → GP), and escalates to specialists. Source: Abu Dhabi primary-care pathways + PURA app workflows (DRAFT). Single source: data/care-pathways.json. Each threshold is reconciled to the canonical ranges (resolver); divergences are flagged.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
IEAT lifecycle
Identify search & stratify the cohort · Engage invite, prepare, lifestyle brief · Assess clinician review & optimise · Transform sustain, monitor & escalate. The app/agent assists each stage (prevention & engagement); clinicians make the decisions (hybrid human-in-the-loop).

Hypertension

Persona — Meera, 49: Perimenopausal, newly-found high BP, low activity; recent benign breast-lump scare.

Stratifier: BP reading · end-organ damage (renal, LVH) · co-morbidities

PriorityOwnerCriteria
P1FMClinic BP ≥180/120; ABPM/HBPM ≥175/115
P2FMClinic BP ≥150/95 (ABPM/HBPM ≥145/90); BAME + co-morbidity & clinic ≥140/90 (ABPM/HBPM ≥135/85); No BP reading in 18 months
P3GP<80y: clinic <140/90 (ABPM/HBPM <135/85); ≥80y: clinic <150/90 (ABPM/HBPM <145/85)
IdentifyEngageAssessTransform
  • Nurse identifies per criteria; prioritise H/M/L
  • Invite via SMS/telephone, explain review
  • Care Coordinator: lifestyle brief (diet/exercise/smoking/weight/alcohol)
  • Nurse: care plan, phlebotomy if bloods due, record BP & BMI
  • GP (P3): review side-effects, contra-indications, CVD risk, lipid + antihypertensive meds
  • FM (P1/2): specialist review as needed, adjust meds, review CVD risk
  • Behaviour change (motivational interviewing)
  • Ongoing BP education; adjust care plan; allocate next review by control & risk

Referral / escalation: Retinal haemorrhage/papilloedema · BP uncontrolled on 4 antihypertensives with confirmed adherence

Monitoring: Home BP diary · Renal, lipids, ACR, ECG as needed · QRisk → lipid-lowering if >10% and not on statin

UAE specifics: BAME-adjusted ≥140/90 priority cut · Cites NICE NG136

Referral / escalation: Specialist diabetic review (uncontrolled/complications) · Retinal screening referral if indicated

Monitoring: HbA1c, eGFR, lipids, LFTs (if statin), urinary ACR · Retinal screening · QRisk → statin if >10% · SGLT2i safety: DKA / Fournier's / UTI; sick-day rules

Chronic Kidney Disease

Persona — Mariam, 63: Poorly-controlled T2DM (HbA1c 9.7%), long-standing HTN, OA knee pain; eGFR fell 72→59, ACR 3.6; anxious after a friend mentioned dialysis.

Stratifier: eGFR + ACR (KDIGO G/A staging) · co-morbidities

PriorityOwnerCriteria
P1FMeGFR <30 or rapid decline ≥25% / ≥15 mL/min in 12m; ACR >70 mg/mmol not on treatment; Resistant hypertension or anaemia
P2FMeGFR 30-59 with ACR >30 or T2DM; eGFR 60-89 with ACR ≥3 or other risk factors
P3GPeGFR >90, ACR <3; Other signs of kidney damage (e.g. polycystic kidneys)
IdentifyEngageAssessTransform
  • Identify CKD 3-5 coded + search uncoded by parameters
  • Classify by eGFR + CKD risk stratification (H/M/L)
  • Invite to 'kidney health review'
  • Nurse: BP, weight, BMI, bloods if >12m; teach home BP & sick-day rules; lifestyle for metabolic health
  • Care Coordinator: schedule follow-up by CKD class
  • GP (P3): review results, medication review
  • FM (P1/2): optimise meds, review anaemia/bone-mineral, nephrology referral; check nephrotoxics; statin (LFT-safe)
  • Stable CKD via metabolic optimisation; fewer AKI admissions via sick-day rules
  • Complete specialist-review actions

Referral / escalation: eGFR <30 or rapidly declining · ACR ≥70 (unless T2DM already treated) · ACR ≥30 + haematuria · CKD + HTN resistant to 4 agents · structural/genetic CKD

Monitoring: eGFR <15 → 3m; 15-29 + ACR>30 → 4m; 30-59 + ACR>30 → 6m · FBC, HbA1c, lipids, LFTs, bone profile, urine dip · Renal ultrasound if eGFR <30

UAE specifics: Malaffi EMR diagnosis coding · Sick-day rules to cut AKI admissions

Dyslipidaemia

Persona — Abdullah, 51: Strong FHx premature CVD (father MI 60, brother 58), BMI 32, treated HTN, ex-smoker; TC 6.2, LDL 3.8, HDL 1.5, TG 2.0; non-diabetic.

Stratifier: 10-year/lifetime CV risk · inherited dyslipidaemia · secondary-prevention eligibility

PriorityOwnerCriteria
P1FMSevere hyperlipidaemia; Suboptimal response / statin-intolerant
P2FM10-year CV risk >10%; Secondary-prevention eligible, or CKD / DMT1
P3GPNo CV-risk assessment completed; Not meeting lipid target; Stable on medication
IdentifyEngageAssessTransform
  • Search eligible for CV-risk assessment (age/co-morbidities) + severe/inherited dyslipidaemia + secondary-prevention candidates
  • Care Coordinator: invite for CV-risk assessment; send lipid-lowering info (diet, lifestyle, risks/benefits)
  • Book high-CV no-comorbidity → GP; secondary-prevention optimisation → FM
  • GP: 10-yr/lifetime CV risk (UAE-validated), screen inherited dyslipidaemia, confirm secondary-prevention eligibility
  • FM: optimise treatment plans for co-morbid/secondary-prevention
  • Offer lipid-lowering for primary & secondary prevention
  • Genetic testing + cascade screening for inherited dyslipidaemia
  • Reduce primary & secondary CV events

Referral / escalation: Lipid clinic if TC >7.5, LDL >4.9, non-HDL >7.5, TG >10 (mmol/L)

Monitoring: Lipid profile + FHx (inherited?) · Re-check factors that under-estimate risk · Shared decision-making tool with individualised risk illustration

UAE specifics: UAE-validated CV risk calculator (not Framingham/QRisk directly)

Obesity

Persona — Omar: Living with obesity; informed of metabolic risk; seeking weight-management support.

Stratifier: BMI · co-morbidities · mobility

PriorityOwnerCriteria
P1FMBMI ≥40, or ≥35 with any comorbidity (uncontrolled T2DM, HTN, OSA, NAFLD, mobility limitation)
P2FMBMI ≥35 with ≥1 comorbidity, or ≥30 with early metabolic risk (prediabetes, HTN)
P3GPBMI ≥25 with no major comorbidities
IdentifyEngageAssessTransform
  • Clinical search for weight-reduction-review candidates; prioritise H/M/L
  • Care Coordinator: assess lifestyle; record weight/height/BMI/waist/BP; bloods (HbA1c, lipids, LFTs, TFTs); 24h dietary recall, activity, smoking, sleep; smoking-cessation & alcohol advice
  • Nurse: care plan, phlebotomy if due
  • GP (BMI ≥25, no major comorbidity): CVD risk, lifestyle, consider pharmacotherapy referral
  • FM (BMI >30+comorbidity or >40): exclude organic (hypothyroid), OSA, obesogenic-drug review, specialist/bariatric referral
  • MDT plan incl. dietitian; review 4-6 weekly until stable then 3-6 monthly
  • Track BMI trajectory, waist, metabolic params; relapse-prevention + digital check-ins

Referral / escalation: Specialist weight-management (pharmacotherapy / bariatric surgery) · OSA assessment · Psychological therapies

Monitoring: Weight, BMI, waist circumference, metabolic parameters · Adjust obesogenic & dose-sensitive meds as weight falls (avoid hypotension/hypoglycaemia)

UAE specifics: High regional obesity & T2DM prevalence framing

Prediabetes — PURA prevention pathway

Persona — —: Auto-enrolled PURA user with HbA1c 5.7-6.4% on prevention track.

Stratifier: HbA1c band + BMI + HTN + FHx + GDM/PCOS/MetS

PriorityOwnerCriteria
HighFMHbA1c 6.3-6.4%; Previous GDM; Obesity BMI ≥35; Metabolic syndrome; PCOS

Referral / escalation: Dietitian (high-risk / worsening) · Physician alert + T2 Diabetes pathway on progression

UAE specifics: PURA app auto-enrollment + dashboard · Prevention Score composite (see prevention-nudges.json)
