Appendix G — Questionnaire Coverage Audit (product-loop lens · cumulative)

A cumulative, versioned evaluation of the lifestyle intake — the validated PROs (Appendix C), the 350-item question bank (Appendix E) and the axes/archetypes (Appendix F) — judged on whether the captured data closes the product loop: capture → score → wearable-match → persona → goals → nudge → adherence → back. Findings are dated; each keeps its full history (open a finding’s history to view & select older dated versions). Numbers compute live from data/question-bank.json.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

HRV (RMSSD) — calculation

IndividualSingle overnight RMSSD from beat-to-beat RR intervals during stable sleep; ms; reject motion/arrhythmia windows.
AggregatedNightly value = median of clean 5-min windows; report a 7-day EWMA to damp night-to-night noise.
DerivedRMSSD = √(mean(ΔRR²)); ln-transform for scoring; autonomic-load companion blends with resting HR.
BaselinePersonal baseline = trimmed 30-night mean ± SD; z = (x−μ)/σ feeds Stage-2b (κ·tanh(z/2), Doc 03 §2b).

Resting HR — calculation

IndividualLowest stable HR during sleep/inactivity; bpm; exclude wake/motion.
AggregatedDaily = sleeping-HR minimum or 10th-pct; 7-day median for trend.
DerivedUsed raw; combined with HRV for the autonomic/stress companion (Doc 12 §4.1).
Baseline30-day personal baseline; β-blocker/illness flagged as confounders (down-weighted).

Sleep efficiency — calculation

IndividualPer-session = time-asleep ÷ time-in-bed; %.
AggregatedNightly value; 7-night mean + regularity (onset-time SD) tracked separately.
DerivedComposite of stage timing; regularity = SD of mid-sleep time over the window.
BaselinePersonal baseline per night-type (work vs free day); ISI corroborates (Appendix C).

Overnight SpO₂ — calculation

IndividualPer-sample reflectance SpO₂; %; consumer-tier accuracy.
AggregatedNightly mean + minimum + desaturation index (ODI).
DerivedODI = desaturation events/hr; flags possible OSA → ISI / clinical PSG.
BaselinePersonal nightly baseline; sustained low → clinical confirmation before any red (D22).

Steps / MVPA — calculation

IndividualPer-epoch accelerometer counts → steps; cadence ≥100 spm ≈ moderate.
AggregatedDaily steps; weekly MVPA minutes (bouts ≥10 min).
DerivedMVPA derived from cadence/HR zones; cross-checked vs IPAQ-SF self-report.
BaselinePersonal 28-day baseline; goal pacing vs target (Goals catalogue).

VO₂max (est.) — calculation

IndividualNot measured directly on consumer devices; inferred — inferential tier.
AggregatedPeriodic estimate; report trend not absolute.
DerivedDerived from HR–pace during runs (firmware model); CPET is the lab reference.
BaselinePersonal trend baseline; absolute level informational only (D22).

eGFR — calculation

IndividualComputed from serum creatinine (± cystatin-C), age, sex; mL/min/1.73m².
AggregatedPer-draw; trend across draws (slope = decline rate).
DerivedCKD-EPI 2021 (race-free); cystatin-C variant when available.
BaselinePersonal slope vs prior draws; pregnancy/elderly frames shift expectation (Doc 05).

ApoB / non-HDL — calculation

IndividualSingle immunoassay (ApoB) or calculated non-HDL = TC − HDL; mg/dL.
AggregatedPer-draw; trend across draws under therapy.
DerivedApoB preferred particle-count proxy; med-responsiveness modelled (modifiability).
BaselinePersonal target vs guideline (e.g. <80 high-risk); FH = low modifiability (Doc 12).

Blood pressure — calculation

IndividualSingle cuff reading; mmHg; clinical-grade cuff = clinical tier.
AggregatedAverage of ≥2 readings/sitting; 7-day home-BP mean (preferred).
DerivedMAP, pulse-pressure derived; white-coat / masked detected vs clinic.
BaselinePersonal home baseline; medication context (treated-to-target, D3).

HbA1c — calculation

IndividualSingle lab assay; % (mmol/mol); reflects ~90-day glycemia.
AggregatedPer-draw; trend across draws; CGM TIR cross-check when present.
DerivedEstimates mean glucose; discordance with CGM flags hemoglobinopathy/turnover.
BaselinePersonal trajectory; pre-DM/T2D thresholds; Ramadan/anemia frames adjust.
