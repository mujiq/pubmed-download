PureScore — by sex

Sex-specific reference cut-points, markers, pillar emphases and sex-gated questions. The shared engine is in the Overview; sex handling is Doc 08.

The male view of the scoring engine: sex-specific reference cut-points, male-specific markers, pillar-weight emphases and the 6 male-gated questions. The shared engine (formula, reservoirs, companion vector) is in the Overview; sex handling is Doc 08.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Pillar emphasis (Male)
CV carries earlier baseline risk; ENDO tracks testosterone/andropause; prostate (PSA) screening; HEM watches polycythemia. Hemoglobin/eGFR/urate read against male reference.

Life-stage frames

Andropause — gradual testosterone decline from midlife; no cyclical hormonal frame.

Male-gated questions (6)

From the question bank, served only when sex = male:

  • END-007 — Have you been diagnosed with or told you have low testosterone (hypogonadism)?
  • END-017 — How often do you experience morning erections (nocturnal penile tumescence)?
  • END-018 — Over the past 4 weeks, how would you rate your sex drive and overall energy level?
  • END-019 — Have you experienced a combination of reduced muscle mass, increased body fat (especially belly), low mood, or reduced stamina over the past year?
  • END-027 — Has a semen analysis or fertility workup revealed any abnormality (e.g. low count, poor motility)?
  • DRM-026 — How would you describe your current hair loss pattern?

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Pillar emphasis (Female)
ENDO dominates across cycle → perimenopause → menopause; BCM bone loss post-menopause (BMD red sooner); HEM iron/menstrual-loss sensitivity; CV risk rises post-menopause. Hemoglobin/HDL/waist read against female reference.

Life-stage frames

Menstrual cycle (follicular/luteal phase-aware ranges) · pregnancy frame (physiologic shifts; pre-eclampsia/GDM anchors retained) · perimenopause/menopause (vasomotor, bone, CV).

Female-gated questions (21)

From the question bank, served only when sex = female:

  • CMB-028 — Have you gone through menopause (natural or surgical), and if so, are you on hormone replacement therapy (HRT)?
  • RHP-010 — How many urinary tract infections (UTIs) have you had in the past 12 months?
  • RHP-029 — Do you currently have any of these symptoms: burning on urination, frequency, urgency, cloudy or foul-smelling urine?
  • RHP-032 — Have you had multiple pregnancies, used oral contraceptives, or undergone rapid weight loss (>1 kg/week)?
  • HEM-001 — How heavy is your typical menstrual flow?
  • HEM-008 — Are you currently pregnant or have you given birth in the past 6 months?
  • HEM-019 — Are you currently using combined oral contraceptives (COC), the contraceptive patch, vaginal ring, or hormone replacement therapy (HRT)?
  • END-003 — How regular are your menstrual cycles?
  • END-004 — Have you been diagnosed with polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)?
  • END-005 — What is your current menopause status?
  • END-006 — Do you experience hot flushes / flashes or night sweats?
  • END-016 — Do you experience hirsutism (excess facial / body hair) or persistent acne related to hormones?
  • END-026 — Have you noticed a significant decrease in sexual desire or libido over the past 3 months?
  • END-029 — Have you or your partner been diagnosed with a cause of female-factor subfertility (e.g. blocked tubes, endometriosis, uterine abnormality)?
  • MSK-005 — Did you experience menopause before age 45 (natural or surgical), or are you currently perimenopausal or postmenopausal?
  • MSK-021 — Are you currently taking or have you taken hormone replacement therapy (HRT / MHT — estrogen ± progesterone)?
  • SLP-017 — Do you experience night sweats or hot flushes that wake you from sleep?
  • DRM-008 — Are your migraines most frequent around your menstrual period or when hormones shift (e.g. at ovulation, with hormonal contraceptives, or in perimenopause)?
  • DRM-011 — Do your acne breakouts worsen in the week before your period?
  • DRM-014 — How would you describe your current hair thinning or shedding pattern?
  • DRM-022 — Have you been diagnosed with iron deficiency or anaemia in the past 2 years?
