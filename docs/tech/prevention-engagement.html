Prevention & Engagement · PURA nudges, alerts & outcomes

The preventive layer that keeps patients on a pathway between clinic touch-points: reminders, a behavioural-intervention catalogue, the alert framework, the condition-level Prevention Score, and the KPI/outcome measures. Reminders should route through the Doc 11 §9 delivery engine, not a parallel sender. Single source: data/prevention-nudges.json.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.

Alert framework

AudienceAlerts
patientTest/appointment due & overdue · High-risk result coaching prompt
care_teamWorsening trend / non-engagement → Care Coordinator monitoring flag · Dietitian-referral suggestion (high-risk / worsening)
physicianProgression to diabetes (HbA1c ≥6.5% / FPG ≥126) → high-priority physician alert + complication screening within 3 months
CDS rule · CDS-PRE-03 — Progression to Diabetes
IF HbA1c ≥6.5% / or FPG ≥126 mg/dL THEN Remove from Prediabetes Pathway · Enroll in Type 2 Diabetes Pathway · High-priority physician alert · Confirmatory HbA1c in 3 months if clinically required · Baseline complication screening (kidney, eye, cardiovascular) within 3 months · Assign care-coordinator follow-up

Prevention Score (condition-level)

A condition-specific composite shown on the PURA prediabetes dashboard — distinct from the whole-person PureScore. Candidate to derive FROM PureScore inputs rather than as a parallel score.

Components: HbA1c status · Weight change · Activity completion · Appointment compliance
