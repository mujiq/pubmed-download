PureScore — System at a glance

One map of the whole platform for a clinical board: how a lab value, a wearable reading or a questionnaire becomes a safe, personalized, governed recommendation. The same seven modules are shown three ways — Flow (how data becomes guidance), Trust & Safety (where the guardrails sit), and Modules (how it is built). Everything below is one diagram you filter, not eight you reconcile.

How to read this
Colour = module, and it stays the same across all three views, so you learn it once: intake, engine (scoring + interpretation), action (nudges & care plan), care delivery (pathways & roles), safety, governance and outcome. Use the View tabs to switch the story, and the Show chips to peel a layer off (e.g. hide the engine internals to see just the patient journey). Each module links to its detailed page underneath.
Illustrative
This is a design model for orientation and discussion — not a shipped architecture or a clinical sign-off. Module boundaries and flows must be reconciled with the production system and re-verified before any clinical or production use (README §5.6).

EngineTurns markers into pillar scores and the overall PureScore, runs the reservoir dynamics, then interprets with the companion vector (confidence, trajectory, early-warning) and personalizes by sex, life-stage and condition.Scoring math, reservoirs, personalizationCalculation Explorer · Doc 03 · Doc 04 · Doc 05
ActionSelects the daily top-5 actions (impact-attributed, ease-weighted, safe, diverse) and delivers them respectfully — quiet hours, caps, opt-out — as nudges and care-plan steps.Action catalogue, nudge ranking & deliveryDoc 11 · Doc 12 · Engagement machines
Care deliveryTurns the score into managed care: activates a condition pathway, stratifies the patient into a 3-tier priority routed to a care-team role (Coordinator / Nurse / FM / GP), runs the Identify→Engage→Assess→Transform lifecycle, monitors between visits and escalates to specialists.Condition pathways, priority routing, care-team roles, preventionCare pathways · Care team & roles · Prevention & engagement

From modules to build

The Modules view above is the bridge to the engineering set: each module maps to one or more services in the C4 architecture, whose runtime objects are the class model, persisted per the data model (ERD), exposed through the API contracts, and choreographed in the runtime sequences. The whole-patient state that all of this drives lives in the patient life-state machine.

What the board should take away
  1. Nothing moves the score without provenance. Self-reports carry lower weight than devices, devices lower than labs, and missing data widens uncertainty rather than guessing.
  2. Safety is a separate, guaranteed channel. It is not "a high-priority nudge" — it bypasses engagement rules and escalates to a human if unacknowledged.
  3. The loop is closed and audited. Every recommendation is checked for adherence, every transition is append-only logged, and the model is versioned and signed off.
  4. Wellness and pricing are firewalled. The actuarial layer is gated and consented separately and never reaches back into the clinical score.
