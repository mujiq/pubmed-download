Appendix D — Personas & Edge Cases

The cohort/edge-case personas that exercise the engine — each with age, medications, representativeness rep (drives Confidence), pillar weight multipliers, and the interpretation frame they test. Used by Doc 08, Doc 18 and the live calculator.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (README §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
PersonaAgeMedsrepWeight multipliersFrame it tests
Healthy adult
healthy		40—0.90—Well-represented reference cohort.
Pre-diabetes
prediabetic		52—0.85MET 1.6 · CV 1.3 · REN 1.1Glycemic burden accumulating; metabolic markers trending up.
CKD-3 on ACE-i
ckd		60ACE-i0.80REN 2.0 · CV 1.4 · MET 1.2BP/UACR managed; mild K⁺ rise drug-expected (Watch, not Alert); eGFR declining.
Endurance athlete
athlete		30—0.40—RHR 42 & low glucose are adaptive; out-of-cohort → cohort stats suppressed, confidence lowered.
On β-blocker
betablocker		58β-blocker0.70CV 1.3RHR & VO₂max confounded (down-weighted, not scored as fitness); BP managed.
Older adult (84)
elderly		84ACE-i0.60REN 1.2 · BCM 1.3 · FIT 1.1Age-adjusted BMI/BP(J-curve)/eGFR/VO₂max frames; low-yield flags de-prioritized; sarcopenic drift; NUT competing-risk-muted.
ESRD on dialysis
dialysis		64—0.50REN 1.5 · CV 1.4eGFR excluded (adequacy frame); structural damage fixed; renal anemia expected; NUT/SLP competing-risk-muted.
Pregnancy (T2)
pregnancy		31—0.50ENDO 1.3 · HEM 1.2 · CV 1.2Physiologic anemia & eGFR-rise expected (bands shifted); pre-eclampsia/GDM absolute anchors retained.
Postmenopausal
menopause		56—0.82CV 1.2 · BCM 1.3Estrogen-loss CV & bone-loss risk (BMD red sooner); ApoB drift; vasomotor sleep impact.
South-Asian male
southasian		45—1.0MET 1.4 · CV 1.3WHO Asian BMI/waist cut-points; ASCVD under-predicts → ethnicity-aware equation; G6PD/FH/vit-D screening raised (D18).
Gulf-Arab T2D, Ramadan
ramadan_dm		52metformin · SGLT2i1.0MET 1.5Med-timing & hypo/dehydration safety (IDF-DAR); SGLT2i held on dehydration risk; clinician-set fast-break rule.
Familial hypercholesterolemia
fh		38—0.75CV 1.5ApoB genetic, NOT lifestyle-modifiable (needs medication); modifiability low.
Lab artifact (hemolysis)
labartifact		55—0.85—Isolated implausible K⁺ 6.6 → reconfirm, not emergency (Doc 05 §3.4); corroboration would escalate.
