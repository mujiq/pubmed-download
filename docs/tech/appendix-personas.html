Appendix D — Personas & Edge Cases

The cohort/edge-case personas that exercise the engine — each with age, medications, representativeness rep (drives Confidence), pillar weight multipliers, and the interpretation frame they test. Used by Doc 08, Doc 18 and the live calculator.

Illustrative
All bands, weights and cut-points on this page are literature/guideline-anchored examples and must be re-verified and cohort-adjusted before any production use (Conventions §5.6). This is design documentation, not a validated medical device.
Clinical pathway personas
The condition-anchored personas (Mariam · CKD, Abdullah · dyslipidaemia, Meera · hypertension, Fatima · mental health, Hala · T2 diabetes, Omar · obesity) live with their care pathway on Condition care pathways (canonical in data/care-pathways.json) — they drive the Identify→Engage→Assess→Transform delivery model.
